Nearby Demand Finder

Paste an Airbnb, VRBO, Vacasa, Zillow or Realtor.com link, or type an address or town. You'll see the ski areas, lakes, restaurants and things to do within a real drive time of the property.

How it works

  • Finds the property using OpenStreetMap's address search. If it can't find the exact house, it tells you whether it matched the street or only the town. You can drag the pin on the map to fix it.
  • Pulls real mapped places from OpenStreetMap in 15 categories: ski areas, beaches, boat launches, restaurants, breweries, golf, grocery, hospitals and more.
  • Measures actual driving time on the road network, not a straight-line circle. A lake between you and a ski hill counts. Times are estimates without traffic.
  • Scores the location by how many of the categories have at least one place within your drive-time cutoff. It's like a report card with one box per category: the score is how many boxes are checked. Nothing in it is guessed.

If you paste a rental listing link (Airbnb, VRBO, Vacasa), a second box appears. Type the town shown on the listing there, because those sites hide the exact address.

The map fills in after a search. You can drag the pin to correct the property location.
Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Address search: Nominatim. Drive times: OSRM, on the OpenStreetMap road network without traffic.
OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers, so it can miss newer businesses or still list closed ones. Check anything important, like a restaurant you plan to name in a listing, before you rely on it.
